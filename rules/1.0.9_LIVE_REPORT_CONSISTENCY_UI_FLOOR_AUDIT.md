## Required behavior
1. Reconcile the preload snapshot against the complete live index before first committed render. Missing report payloads are staged off-state and committed atomically; partial live fetches never leak into Reports or statistics.
2. Reports, metrics and statistical/coverage views share the same committed report Map.
3. Reports show server-authoritative submission Date, Time and Time zone as separate lines.
4. Reports replace the truncated ID text with a control that copies the exact 64-character report ID without opening the row.
5. Coverage below 20% is visually patterned/angled, with an additional very-low treatment below 5%, while retaining exact numerical widths and semantic state colors.
6. New submissions from 1.0.18 and every older VulkanScope version are rejected. 1.0.19 and newer remain subject to the full existing schema/registry/security contract. Historical stored reports remain readable.

## Non-regression boundary
No D1 migration, report/hash rewrite, schema bump, normalizer bump, registry corpus change, payload-storage change, transport-limit weakening, CORS weakening or personal-data-policy weakening is authorized by this release.
